# Product

## Register

brand + product (hybrid: high-impact brand landing page + precision cryptographic audit dashboard)

## Platform

web

## Users

1. **Autonomous Agent Operators & DAO Treasurers**: Entities deploying autonomous capital and delegating business operations (invoicing, bookkeeping, procurement) to external AI agents. They cannot manually inspect thousands of invoices, but cannot afford wallet-draining hacks.
2. **CFOs, Financial Controllers & Accountants**: Teams seeking automated verification of supplier billing, strict VAT compliance (Czech VAT 21%/12%/0%), and fraud prevention before funds leave their custody.
3. **Hackathon Evaluators & Web3 Developers**: Judges and builders at the *From Dusk Till Dawn #01 Hackathon* (Theme: Agentic Economy, Partner: Masumi Network) evaluating verifiable autonomous B2B commerce on Cardano.

## Product Purpose

Enable verifiable, economically accountable B2B commerce between AI agents:
A client buyer agent discovers service providers (accounting firms), locks escrow in Cardano smart contracts via Masumi Network, receives extracted financial documents, independently validates mathematical accuracy and business registries (ARES), and either settles payment or cryptographically disputes bad work—automatically recovering funds and slashing seller reputation.

### Non-negotiable principles:
- **No trust without cryptographic escrow**: Pre-paying external agents is prohibited; funds are locked on-chain before work begins.
- **Hard wallet policies in code**: Limits, per-task caps, human approval thresholds, and duplicate detection are strictly enforced in deterministic software, not LLM prompts.
- **Independent multi-layer audit**: Math, tax rates, and registries are validated independently. Prompt injections in document payloads cannot manipulate payment amounts.
- **No AI slop**: The interface must reflect forensic cryptographic rigor—restrained, precise, tabular, high-contrast, without generic AI templates.

## Positioning

"B2B AI Agents Trade. Smart Contracts Enforce."
The forensic escrow layer for the autonomous agentic economy.

## Brand Personality

- **Forensic**: Sharp, mathematical, unyielding, evidentiary.
- **Cryptographic**: Verifiable hashes, on-chain truth, deterministic state transitions.
- **High-craft utility**: Engineered like a Bloomberg terminal or mission-critical vault monitor, not a generic SaaS landing page.

## Anti-References

- Generic neon-cyberpunk Web3 DEXs with glowing purple grids.
- Template SaaS pages with 3 identical cards, big gradient numbers, and "01 / 02 / 03" tracked kicker scaffolding.
- Fluffy AI landing pages full of vague promises ("supercharge your workflow") and soft pastel blobs.
